## Conventions and constraints
- Private. Contains personal details; don't share outside the people involved (lawyer, authorities if the user decides).
- The statement only records what the user said. Unknown details stay as `[...]`; don't fill them in.
- Location: foot of the Hoàng Hoa Thám flyover at the Cộng Hòa junction; the turn is into road A4.
- Facts the user stated (second, detailed account; it replaces the first short one): U-turn from Cộng Hòa onto Hoàng Hoa Thám toward Lăng Cha Cả with the left signal on; right signal on about 30 to 40 m before A4 with right-mirror checks; moved to about 50 cm from the right kerb; lightly braked about 2 m before A4 at about 5 km/h (the user's estimate) and saw no one behind on the right; heard a bike rev behind while turning and could not check the mirror again; felt a touch at the rear with a bang; was already fully in A4, panicked (feared being beaten by a crowd), and kept going without stopping. Does not know the other rider's condition. The statement keeps that sequence, including not stopping and the reason.
- Clarified by the user: the other bike came from behind; the user did not see anyone on the right (already near the kerb); "reckless overtaking" is only the user's guess. The statement separates what was seen from what was guessed. Don't present the guess as a fact.
- Clarified by the user: only one bang was heard; the user does not know whether the other rider fell (the first account had said the rider fell). The statement records one bang and "don't know whether they fell". Don't present a fall as a fact.
- Vehicle damage: exhaust guard (gác bảo vệ pô) has a small piece broken at the rear end. Photos to follow.
